### Reflections
i try and try and try,  
i often fool myself into thinking,  
the person infront of me,  
they're the same as me!  
  
a lover tries,  
a lover is kind,  
a lover is compassionate!  
  
then I relise,  
that's just my reflection.  
  

### Attachment
i have this belief,  
the more i love,  
the more i will be loved.  
  
i'm always thinking,  
how can i do better?  
how can i be better,  
so the next person doesn't leave me?  
  
how much control,  
how little, 
do i have?
over whether i 'deserve' love?  
  
<div align="right">
do i have none at all?  
</div>
of course not.  
are there any guarantees?  
not at all.  
  
how do i convince myself,  
i deserve love?  
when i have been judged that i don't?  
are you sure that's the truth?  
no. 

the truth might have nothing to do with you,  
or everything.  
how does it feel when you,  
can't meet someone where they are?  
anxiety inducing.  
not living up to their expectations.  

have you thought you could love too much?  
perhaps.  
and how would someone express their anxiety?  
"you're too good for me".  
does that sound familiar?  
yes.
