### Reflections
i try and try and try,  
i often fool myself into thinking,  
the person infront of me,  
they're the same as me!  
  
a lover tries,  
a lover is kind,  
a lover is compassionate!  
  
then I relise,  
that's just my reflection.  
  

### Attachment
i believe,  
the more i love,  
the more i will be loved.  
  
i'm always thinking,  
how can i do better,  
how can i be better,  
so the next person doesn't leave me?  
  
should i be more selfless?  
give up every part of my being for them?  

is it possible to love too much?  
to overwhelm someone,  
to make them feel as though their love is inadequate?  
like you're bringing the sun,  
while they're bringing a candle.  



### moments   
when you're in the midst of it,  
you don't notice them.  
then you're at the end,  
watching now fade into then.  
  
a moment sparks joy in you,  
you go to share it,  
you realise,  
there is noone to send it to.  
  
nearest the end of your past,  
many moments are filled with thoughts of them,  
as time grows,  
less and less moments are spent back then.  
  
months later,  
a moment might strike you,  
"this is when we planned to be together"  
what was once a now,  
is now a never.  
  
a year later you realise,  
there have been more moments,  
between now and then,  
than the pasts starts and ends.
